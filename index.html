About Cleaniac

Everyday cleaning essentials, simply sourced

Cleaniac offers a growing range of practical cleaning and maintenance solutions for everyday household and commercial requirements. From floor and kitchen care to laundry, automotive cleaning and larger-volume packs, the range is designed to make sourcing everyday cleaning essentials simpler.

Cleaniac is a unit of MAS Group and products are offered in both household retail packs and larger commercial quantities, so the same range can support a single home or an entire facility.

Yes. Add products to the cart and send the complete order through WhatsApp.

Products with multiple fragrance options will display the available selections before adding to cart.
How do I confirm my order?
